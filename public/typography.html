Can't install Systemia, like in Google Slides or system apps? Use IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono from Google Fonts, or Helvetica Neue and SF Mono as system fallbacks.

Type scale

SemiBold headings carry the hierarchy from 120 down to 26pt, with Medium variants for titles inside cards and panels. Tight 90% leading and negative tracking give headlines presence. Body copy opens up on a 4px rhythm so information stays easy to scan. Sizes in brackets are mobile.

Setting type

Range text left. Center only short statements meant to make an impact, or narrow formats. Separate paragraphs with a line space, never an indent. Set headlines in sentence case.
