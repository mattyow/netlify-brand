08 · Motion

Motion

Motion brings the joy of making into the interface. Elements respond, rearrange and settle with ease, connecting each action to its result.

Principles

Every animation should do one of these four jobs. If it doesn't, cut it.

Magical

Reveal possibility

Brief color reveals and unexpected continuity between states.

Mighty

Move with purpose

Confident transitions settle decisively and keep the interface ready for real work.

Human

Respond warmly

Small lifts, soft acceleration and restrained spring motion feel approachable.

Guiding

Show what changed

Motion connects the action to its result and keeps the next step clear.

Easing

Easing describes the character of movement. Duration describes its pace. Keep them separate, then pair them in presets. Expressive spring motion is reserved for illustration and success moments. Press play to compare.

Directcubic-bezier(0.20, 0, 0, 1)Immediate response, clean finish.
Settlecubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)Soft arrival without overshoot.
Standardcubic-bezier(0.40, 0, 0.2, 1)Continuous change between states.

Duration

Choose timing by how far the object moves. Under 10px, use Short. Up to 600px, use Subtle. Anything larger, like full-screen transitions, uses Gentle.
