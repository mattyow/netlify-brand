Illustration

Where Netlify shows the people behind the product: stacking, reaching, arranging screens and shapes into something new.

A brand moment, not decoration

Our scenes are set in bright, painterly worlds lit by the gradient palette, so the human side of the brand and the color system feel like one thing. Illustration leads in the hero and returns in the closing section, framing the main action rather than competing with it. Everywhere else, texture and gradients stay restrained, and text and UI always sit on clear backgrounds.

Complexity follows size: full scenes for heroes, a single figure or object for spots, texture alone for the smallest shapes.

Types

The illustration library is still in progress. Final artwork and source files will be shared through locked assets.

Hero illustrations

Full scenes of people creating together, with wide crops, soft light and color drawn from the gradient palette. Use one at the top of a page and one in the closing section. Keep faces and key actions clear of the headline and CTA.

Spot illustrations

Smaller moments for cards, feature blocks and empty states: one person or one object on a simple background, in the same palette and light. They should read at a glance at card size.
