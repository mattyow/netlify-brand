Video call backgrounds

Netlify backgrounds for Google Meet and Zoom. Each one is 1920 × 1080 with the logo top left and open space in the middle where you’ll sit.

Backgrounds

Setting one up

Download a background first. Menu names shift between app versions, so look for the nearest match.

Google Meet

  1. In a call, open the ⋮ menu and choose Apply visual effects.
  2. Under Backgrounds, click the + tile to upload your own.
  3. Pick the downloaded PNG.

Zoom

  1. Open Settings → Background & effects, or in a call click the arrow next to Stop Video.
  2. Under Virtual backgrounds, click + → Add image.
  3. Pick the downloaded PNG.
If the logo looks backwards, that’s only your own preview: video apps mirror your self-view, and everyone else sees it the right way round. To check, turn off Mirror my video in Zoom’s video settings.
