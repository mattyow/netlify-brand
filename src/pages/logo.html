02 · Logo

Logo

Our logo is our stamp of authenticity. Reproduce it consistently so it stays legible and recognizable everywhere it appears.

Logo kit

Use the full lockup in nearly all communications. Use the symbol alone where the Netlify name is already clear, like app icons, avatars and small UI. Always use the supplied artwork files.

Clear space and minimum size

Leave room around the logo equal to the small diamond in the symbol, shown as x. There is no maximum size. Keep the lockup at least 20px tall on screen.

Color and placement

The logo is off black or white, never anything else. The background decides which. On imagery and gradients, lead with white, but switch to off black if white won't read. Place the full lockup in a corner of the composition. Place the symbol on its own only in the center.

Partnership lockups

The width of the Netlify symbol sets the space between the two logos. A geometric cross sits in the center to connect both identities while keeping them clearly separate. Match the partner logo's optical height to ours.

Incorrect usage

Never redraw, recolor or rearrange the logo. These treatments compromise it.
