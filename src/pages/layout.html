05 · Spacing & shape

Spacing & shape

A 4px rhythm, an 8-column grid and a radius scale that grows with the element.

Spacing scale

Steps go up by 4 until 24, then widen to 8, 16 and 32 up to 128, so small gaps can be fine-tuned while large gaps keep a steady rhythm. Reach for semantic tokens first and use the scale for anything they don't cover. Avoid typing raw numbers.

Semantic spacing

Semantic tokens describe what the space is for, not how big it is. They switch between desktop and mobile, so large gaps tighten on small screens automatically.

Grid

8 columns on desktop and 4 on mobile, with 20px gutters. Outer margins are 40px on desktop and 20px on mobile. Content snaps to the columns, and the space around it is left open so each idea has room to breathe.

Corner radius

Radius scales with the size of the element, in steps of 10. The bigger the element, the bigger the radius, and anything you click that is pill-shaped uses full. MD steps down to 20 on mobile.

None · 0Full-bleed images, table cells

XS · 10Tags, tooltips, thumbnails

SM · 20Cards, panels, inputs

MD · 30 (20)Large cards, feature media

Full · 9999Buttons, chips, badges

Elevation

Most surfaces sit flat. Use elevation for things that float above the page: menus, tooltips, the active nav item and modals.
